# Visionary — the Google×Apple product, born complete

## Part 1 · What the full reference study established

Pages studied live: **Apple** — homepage, iPhone 18 Pro (product page), Privacy, Accessibility, Newsroom. **Google** — Store homepage, Privacy Policy, Safety Center, Accessibility, blog.google, Careers app, Google Help (support), Workspace enterprise page.

The laws both companies share (our product must inherit all of them):
1. **Slogan H1s** — "Privacy. That's Apple." / "The better way to work" — never a label, always a claim in one breath.
2. **Local way-finding** — under the global nav: page name + 3–6 anchor links + one action (Apple product/values pages). Long policies get an in-page TOC + "Jump to content".
3. **Tile discipline** — name → one-line tagline → max 2 CTAs → the visual is the largest element. Nothing decorative.
4. **One accent, flat surfaces** — no pastel-tint dialects, no chrome; Google Store's cards are white + hairline + tiny shadow.
5. **Claims → footnotes** — every number or capability claim carries a footnote; Apple ships a footnotes region above the footer.
6. **Trust architecture** — Safety Center pillars + "Learn more" cards; Apple's values strip (Privacy/Accessibility/Education/Environment cross-links) before every footer.
7. **Keyboard-complete** — skip-nav, arrow-navigable menus, visible carousel scrollbars, reduced-motion everywhere.
