## Apple-informed original design rationale

The connected review dialog follows the App Store subscription pattern — the exact recurring
amount, billing term, auto-renewal and cancellation terms are stated on one review surface, an
explicit consent action gates the provider handoff, and cancellation keeps paid access through the
confirmed period. Visionary's implementation remains original: server-owned catalog prices, INR
test-mode labeling throughout, and no copied assets or copy.
