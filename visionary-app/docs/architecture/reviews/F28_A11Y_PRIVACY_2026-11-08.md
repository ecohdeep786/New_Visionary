## Apple-informed original design rationale

Apple Accessibility treats pointer, keyboard and assistive input as first-class: the 44px touch
target with no overlapping hit areas is exactly that principle, applied to Visionary's own 8px dot
language. The cross-engine matrix extends the same honesty to browser engines — the same laws must
hold on WebKit and Firefox, not just Chromium.
