## Defect (T09 research finding, filed as FAP-87)

The dashboard access gate (`DashboardLayout.jsx:100-105`) renders the FAP-75 self-explaining notice **only** for organization areas (`people/cohorts/curriculum/analytics`) and silently rewrites the address bar to `/dashboard/home` for **every other role-restricted section**. Verified live in the T09 research pass (fresh preview accounts for all five identities, viewport 1440, 0 console errors):

- student → `/dashboard/prepare`, `/learners`, `/insights`, `/child`, `/reports`, `/career` — silently redirected to Home ("Welcome back", no explanation)
- teacher → `/dashboard/child`; parent → `/dashboard/prepare`; organization → `/dashboard/child`; professional → `/dashboard/prepare`, `/child` — same silent bounce

This is the "fresh workspace routes redirecting home rather than displaying contextual empty states" pattern the V6 plan's T09 lane flags, and it contradicts the FAP-75 law inside the same gate: the same visit to an organization area keeps its URL and explains itself.

## 1. The change (three files; one law, generalized)

- `frontend/src/lib/dashboardNavigation.js`: new `dashboardAreaOwner(section)` — the role that owns each restricted section outside the organization areas (`prepare/learners/insights/growth/library → teacher`, `child/reports → parent`, `career → professional`, `audit → organization`, `class → teacher`), documented next to `isOrganizationArea`.
- `frontend/src/pages/dashboard/RoleAreaUnavailable.jsx`: generalizes beyond `isOrganizationArea` — the owner comes from the map (organization areas stay organization-owned); the area heading is looked up in the owner's **primary and secondary** navigation (so `audit/growth/library` get their real labels: "Audit", "Teacher Growth", "Library"); the boundary sentence is byte-identical for organization-owned areas ("This workspace area opens with an organization workspace. …") and uses the same shape for the other owners ("… opens with a teaching/family/professional workspace …"); the visitor's own working paths and the localized "Return to workspace" action are unchanged; the component still never navigates.
- `frontend/src/components/dashboard/DashboardLayout.jsx`: the gate now renders the notice for `isOrganizationArea(section) || dashboardAreaOwner(section)`; the `Navigate` fallback survives only for sections outside both maps (unknown-section safety net).

Own-role routes, empty states, and all passing deep links are untouched.
