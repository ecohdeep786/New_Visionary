## Defect (T02 research walk, same date)

A fresh learner asking the Visionary Guide a question in a workspace without a teaching connection received a reply whose status label and first sentence led with backend plumbing — label "Teaching service not connected", body "Visionary Guide's teaching service is not connected." The learner's own outcome (their question was saved) appeared only mid-sentence, and the reply offered no next step at all: a dead-end message with no affordance.
