Task: FAP-77 (epic FAP-69, V6 T02 UX Research & Human Factors) · Candidate on `zcode/fap-77-learn-vocab` (base verified `origin/beta` @ `8fd60c83`) · QA executed 2026-11-09 against the candidate dev server (localhost:5173) and the base checkout server (localhost:5174, beta `8fd60c83`).

## Defect (T02 research walk)

A fresh learner reaching the Learn entry from the dashboard met internal fixture language as the product's own words: the authored-sample task family introduced itself as "Try authored learning sample" (school) / "Try authored workplace sample" (professional), the multi-book path as "Try a multi-book sample", the empty subject state said "…try an authored sample.", and the authored-mathematics view headed itself "Authored mathematics sample". "Sample" is the engineering name for a fixture (SAMPLE_SELECTION / SAMPLE_LIBRARY_SELECTION); a first-time learner cannot be expected to know the product keeps authored demo content. The working paths behind the words exist; the words fail the seat.
