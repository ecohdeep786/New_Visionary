## Auth-mode scope (verified before implementing)

`base44.auth` resolves through two adapters: the **Firebase adapter** (`source === 'firebase'`, live config `infrastructure/gcp/firebase-web-config.json`, project `visionary-research`) and a **local-preview localStorage fallback** that is active whenever `VITE_AUTH_MODE=firebase` is unset. No `.env.local` exists in the checkout, so both dev servers (5174 beta, 5173 candidate) ran the preview fallback — which is the only branch FAP-70's defect lived in. The connected Firebase branch was never affected and its copy stays byte-identical (asserted by the contract test).

Founder direction recorded 2026-11-11: the Firebase-live path is the real product path and connects to the Firebase console. The full **Firebase-live journey verification** (labeled `t08-firebase-*@visionary.test` accounts, verification-email dispatch, verification gate, resend cooldown) is a separate T08 step that follows this merge; the final emailed-link click cannot be exercised from this environment (no mailbox) and will be disclosed as untested there rather than claimed.

## Defect (T08 audit finding, filed as FAP-70)

At beta `67edaa93`, `/register`'s **preview** OTP view claimed **"We sent a 6-digit code to \<email\>"** although preview mode has no email transport; **Resend** showed the toast **"Code sent / Check your email for the new code."** (`resendOtp()` returns `{ok:true}` without any transport); and `verifyOtp` **ignores the code entirely** — entering `000000` completed registration live (`appClient.js:212-233`). The step title falsely read "Verify your email".

Why it matters: an unfulfilled promise to the learner at the most sensitive step of the funnel, and a violation of the project's truthfulness law (no invented behavior). The preview disclosure voice existed elsewhere (onboarding review step) but not at the OTP step.
