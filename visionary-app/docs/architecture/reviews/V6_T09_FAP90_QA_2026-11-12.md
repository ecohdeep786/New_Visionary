## Defect (residual instance of the FAP-87 class; recorded during FAP-89's probes, freshly reproduced)

Deep-linking `/dashboard/classes` from an **organization** identity (and, by the same gate logic, a **parent** identity) silently rewrote the address bar to `/dashboard/home` — URL lost, no explanation. Evidence: `t09-fap89-org-denials.json` and `t09-fap89-postmerge.json` (route `/dashboard/classes` → final `/dashboard/home`, "Welcome back…" home) captured on merged beta `ce2c391a`/`5e9a6e52`.

**Cause:** DashboardLayout gate 1 (`canAccessDashboardPath`) denies the section for identities without a Classes surface (organization, parent) and falls back to `<Navigate to="/dashboard/home" replace />` unless the section is in `isOrganizationArea` or the FAP-87 `areaOwners` map — which mapped `prepare/learners/insights/growth/library/child/reports/career/audit/class` but **not the plural `classes` list route**. A cross-product of every navigation section against every role (now asserted by a contract test) shows `classes` was the **only** remaining section that could be denied at gate 1 without an owner mapping.

## Fix (1 file + tests)

- `src/lib/dashboardNavigation.js`: `areaOwners` gains `classes:'teacher'` (with a comment noting the FAP-90 law). The FAP-75/87 gate then keeps the URL and renders `RoleAreaUnavailable`: heading **"Classes"** (from the teacher's localized navigation), boundary sentence **"This workspace area opens with a teaching workspace. You are currently working in a organization/family workspace."**, the viewer's own primary paths as alternatives, and the return action. No behavior change for student/professional/teacher identities (they pass `canAccessDashboardPath` for `/dashboard/classes` and never reach the branch).
- `tests/fap90-classes-areowner.test.mjs` (new, 4 tests): the owner mapping + label resolution; student/professional/teacher access unchanged; organization/parent still denied at gate 1; and a **no-rewrite-holes** sweep — every section any role can be denied at gate 1 must have an `areaOwners` mapping or an organization-area entry.
- `tests/fap87-restricted-deeplink-explain.test.mjs`: the exact-map contract updated to include `classes:'teacher'` (the FAP-87 map legitimately grew; disclosed here).
